Continental drift describes the movement of the Earth's tectonic plates over geological time, reshaping the surface of the planet.

Supply and demand is the economic model describing how the price of a good is determined by buyer willingness and seller availability.
The speed of light in a vacuum is approximately 299,792 kilometres per second and is a fundamental constant of physics.
Neurons communicate through electrochemical signals called action potentials, transmitted across synapses via neurotransmitters.
The water cycle describes the continuous movement of water through evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and surface runoff.
Natural selection is the mechanism by which heritable traits that increase reproductive success become more common over generations.
The central limit theorem states that the sum of independent random variables tends toward a normal distribution as sample size grows.
