Why Phase A exists

In v1.1, reinforcement is additive: activation += gain. Run the engine long enough and a handful of frequently-encountered concepts become permanently dominant — they crowd out everything new. The system gets cognitively stuck.

Phase A fixes the structural root cause with three interlocking mechanisms: a saturation curve that slows growth as concepts approach full activation, reinforcement fatigue that penalises rapid repetition, and activation cooling that makes hot concepts burn out faster. Together they create cognitive resistance — the system pushes back against fixation automatically.

All three changes live inside meta_state.py [M] and config.yaml. No other files change. No new nodes. No new protocols. Pure behaviour change inside the existing saturation boundary.
